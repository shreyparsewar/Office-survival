# Office Survival 1999 — 20-second promo concepts

## Selected: One Monday

The viewer clocks into the real 3D office, selects a coworker, hands off work, and sees the workday advance. The same green action button carries the eye into the title card. The first two seconds show the crowded office and the 9 AM clock. Beats: clock in → meet the team → assign the work → watch the clock → survive → title. Look: the game's pixel type, cream panels, deep navy borders, and green action color. Sound: a brisk original retro instrumental with sparse click and paper accents.

## Alternative: The deadline

The Q3 report due at 12:10 is the travelling object. The camera starts on the Today card, zooms to a coworker's progress, then pulls back to the branch report. Look: document cards and office screenshots, with a ticking rhythm and a final drum stop.

## Alternative: Ten coworkers

The cast leads the video. Quick match cuts between character closeups introduce teamwork and office interruptions, ending on the full office. Look: character portraits framed by game-native UI, with playful percussion and camera whip transitions.
